Draw an inline SVG diagram in the "illustrated map" style of my Genesis study.

PURPOSE: The diagram must tell the chapter's story at a glance — a reader should
understand the movement without reading the essay.

LAYOUT
- Canvas 560 wide, ~230–330 tall. One idea per diagram.
- Title at top center in small sans-serif caps with wide letter-spacing
  (≈9.5px, tracking 1.8), e.g. "QUESTION · DISTORT · DENY".
- Reads like a map: places laid out in space, usually one journey left → right
  (or a fork / a V / two parallel roads). No tables, no grids of text.
- One big soft region (rounded rect, radius ~28, translucent sage fill, thin
  charcoal outline) for the main "place" (God, Eden, the garden, the door, the city).
- One hero: a single bold filled circle (sage green) as the focal point,
  optionally with short radiating rays for God / light / wisdom / Enoch.
- Stops along the path: small rings (r≈5). Ends that mean death/sin can be
  filled rust dots.
- A thin rule near the bottom, then one closing line.

DRAWINGS (line art, not icons-in-boxes) — use them generously:
tree (round canopy + trunk, rust fruit dots), serpent (S-curve, head, forked tongue),
person (filled head, thick stroke body), fig leaf, eye, thorns, altar with smoke
(rising straight = accepted, drifting dashed = rejected), sheaf of grain, lamb,
walled city, tent, lyre, hammer & anvil, arched door (something crouching at it),
garment, protective mark, drop of blood, sword, waves, rain, ark, grave mound.
Scale drawings large enough to read (≈20–35px), and keep them clear of text.

ARROWS TELL THE STORY
- Green arrow = God's gift / what is received / the promise.
- Rust arrow = loss, blame, sin, judgment. Dashed rust = falls short / turns back.
- Paths can change shape to show meaning (straight → wavy → zigzag as truth bends).

WORDS — FEWER
- Place names in small sans caps (≈7.5–8.5px).
- One short serif italic note under each place (≈10.5px), muted color.
- Verse references short: "3:9", "4:15". No sentences longer than one line.

COLOR (CSS variables so it works in light and dark mode)
- --sage-deep: life, God, what is given.   --rust: loss, sin, curse.
- --charcoal: neutral lines.   --ink: main text.   --muted: notes.
- --sage-soft: region fills.   Background is the panel color; never pure black/white.

CHECK: no text overlaps a drawing, a line, or other text; nothing touches the
edges; it stays legible when scaled to a 375px-wide phone.
